# Comparison Report

The report compares 3 distinct types of households -
 - Couple with resident children households
 - Couple without resident children households
 - Single parent households

It depicts how these 3 different types of households (which is further divided into different groups either according to the age of the youngest child, if the household resident children or according to the oldest person in the household).

The report shows how the total rank (among 15 household) derived by the household income (**where rank 1 is the highest income**)  have changed for these three groups over the observed period of 19 years.
