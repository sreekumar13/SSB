# Comparison Report

This report compares the number of households and after-tax income in constant terms between two different household types:
* Households where a person is living alone but their age is 67 years or older.
* Households where a person is living alone but their age is between 30-44 years.

The reason for these two households to have been picked is that the older age group is generally the age group that is in general considered in their retirement age whereas the younger age group is when a person usually the early stage of their working career.

The report compares how the difference between number of households and the difference between income for these two households have performed, and if the gap between these two households are shrinking or expanding.
