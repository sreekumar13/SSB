# Comparison Report

The report shows a comparison between two distinct subgroups within the broad 15 household types :
* Couple with resident children households
* Single Parent households

These households are further divided into three sub divisions among themselves according to the age group the youngest child belongs to:

* Category 1: 0-5 years old
* Category 2: 6-17 years old
* Category 3: Older than 17 years

The report shows how the percentage difference in after-tax income in constant terms between the household types for the subgroups have been over the observed period of 19 years (2006-2024).
